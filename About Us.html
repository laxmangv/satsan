Our Story

SatSan Capital was founded on a simple belief—every financial journey is unique. We recognized that meaningful financial planning goes beyond numbers and investments; it begins with understanding people, their aspirations, and the life they envision for themselves and their families.

What started by helping friends and family make informed financial decisions gradually evolved into serving a wider community. Today, many of our clients come through referrals—a reflection of the trust, transparency, and lasting relationships we strive to build.

Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)

Non-Convertible Debentures are fixed-income investment instruments issued by companies to raise capital. They provide attractive interest rates and predictable returns while offering investors a diversified income source.

Benefits

  • ✔ Higher interest than traditional Fixed Deposits
  • ✔ Fixed and regular income
  • ✔ Variety of investment tenures
  • ✔ Listed NCDs offer liquidity
  • ✔ Suitable for conservative investors

Who Should Invest?

Investors looking for steady income, capital preservation, and portfolio diversification.
